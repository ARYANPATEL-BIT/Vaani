Vaani

Vaani - a real-time voice agent for Hindi, English and Hinglish

Vaani is a voice agent you talk to out loud. Speak in Hindi, English or a mix of the two, and it answers in speech. You can interrupt it mid-sentence: it stops talking and listens to you.

It listens through your microphone, transcribes what you said, writes a reply with a language model, and speaks the reply back to you, all in a few seconds.

Vaani is built by Aryan Patel as a B.Tech CSE (AI/ML) on-the-job learning project at Polaris School of Technology with Futuretalks Services Private Limited.

Open Vaani

Vaani runs on the developer's own computer, so it is only online while that computer is running it. If the link does not load, it is offline for now.
