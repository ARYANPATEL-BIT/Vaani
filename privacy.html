Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Vaani is a real-time voice agent built by Aryan Patel as a student learning project. This page explains what Vaani collects when you use it, who processes it, and how to have it deleted.

What we collect

  • Your name and email address, from Google sign-in. Vaani never sees your Google password, and it does not ask for or receive any other Google data (no contacts, files, calendar or anything else).
  • A session cookie. After you sign in, Vaani sets one signed, httpOnly cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 7 days. It holds your Google account ID, name, email and the time it expires. Page scripts cannot read it.
  • Microphone audio. While a conversation is running, audio from your microphone is streamed to the Vaani server so it can be transcribed. The audio is not saved.
  • Conversation records. For each conversation, Vaani stores what you said (as text), the replies, and timing measurements (how long each step took) in a database on the developer's computer. These records are used to measure and improve Vaani's speed and quality.

Who processes it

  • Speech recognition and text-to-speech run on the developer's computer. Your audio is not sent to any speech or AI service; it only travels over the ngrok link described below.
  • Writing replies. The text of your messages (and the earlier messages of the same conversation) is sent to Groq and, as a fallback when Groq is unavailable, to Google Gemini, which write the replies. Their privacy policies apply to what they receive.
  • The public link goes through ngrok, which forwards your connection to the developer's computer.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • There are no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.
  • Your data is not used to train any model.

Deleting your data

You can sign out at any time; signing out removes the session cookie from your browser.

To have your conversation records deleted, email aryan.void888@gmail.com. The records are not linked to your account, so please say roughly when you used Vaani, and they will be found and deleted.
